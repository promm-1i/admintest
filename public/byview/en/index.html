Get started

You need one phone to film with and one phone or tablet to watch on. If a reply to your question says "see Monitor 2", it means this number.

Install on both

Get it on Google PlayAndroid 10 or later
  1. 1Install Byview on the camera phone and the viewing deviceThe viewing device can be a phone or a tablet.

Camera phone

  1. 1Open Byview and choose Camera phoneA 6-digit code appears.
  2. 2Set it on a tripod or a standIt films with the back camera.

Monitor

  1. 1Open Byview and choose Monitor
  2. 2Tap the camera phone in the nearby list, or enter the 6-digit codeYou enter the code only the first time. After that, tap it in the list.
  3. 3Tap the picture to focus, press the red button to recordVideo is saved on the camera phone, in the Movies/Byview folder.

Help

The camera phone isn't in the list

Byview has to be open on the camera phone, on the Camera phone screen, for it to appear in the list. If it isn't there, entering the 6-digit code from the camera phone also connects right away.

It says the code doesn't match

Enter the code shown on the camera phone right now. If you changed the code in the camera phone's settings, devices you connected before need the new code once.

Where are my recordings?

On the camera phone, in the Movies/Byview folder. They show up in your gallery app too. Nothing is saved on the viewing device.

The camera phone's screen went dark

20 seconds after a monitor connects, the camera phone's screen dims to save battery; filming goes on. Tap the screen to brighten it, or turn this off in Settings.

Can I try it with one phone first?

Settings → One-phone test shows what this phone is filming on this same phone, like a monitor.
