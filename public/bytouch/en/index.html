Help

Taps arrive late when the screen is off

When the phone puts Bytouch to sleep to save battery, taps can arrive late or not at all. Tap “Allow background activity” in Bytouch's settings, on the phone that receives.

A notification stays at the top

It shows that the connection is kept open so taps arrive while the screen is off. It goes away when you disconnect.

It doesn't vibrate in silent mode

Turn on “Vibrate in silent mode” in settings.

The watch doesn't vibrate

Check that “Also send to watch” is on in the phone's settings and that Bytouch is installed on the watch. After the first 3 days the watch locks unless one of you has bought the unlock.

I got a new phone

Pair again with a code on the new phone. Your purchase stays unlocked on the same Google account.

I want to disconnect

Tap “Disconnect” at the bottom of settings. The pairing is removed from both phones and the lamp; to use it again, pair with a new code.
