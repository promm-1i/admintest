ORVELLA Introduction

ORVELLA Orthopaedic Care

Orvella brings joint assessment, treatment and rehabilitation together, with clear explanations and attention to the circumstances of each patient. Our care is guided by clinical knowledge, careful preparation and respectful communication.

Specialties Advancing Knowledge
in Joint Care

The joint care team at Orvella studies assessment and treatment across
knee replacement, arthroscopic procedures,
sports injuries and approaches that preserve the joint.

Promise Our Commitment
to Excellence

Helping people use their joints in everyday life is the purpose
that guides our clinical discussions and treatment planning.
We continue to review clinical practice and develop knowledge within these orthopaedic fields.

Care That Connects Treatment and Everyday Life, ORVELLA

Orvella Principles

Patient-Centered
Values & Trust

Our approach combines careful clinical practice, ethical decisions
and an understanding of cost, with respect for the individual
and a willingness to listen throughout the course of care.

We take time to hear your concerns and explain the findings
so that treatment choices and the stages of recovery
can be discussed in a setting that feels calm and welcoming.
Welcome to orthopaedic care at Orvella.

Vision A Global Vision
for Connected Joint Care

We aim to develop joint reconstruction and rehabilitation services
that connect careful clinical work in Korea with the exchange
of knowledge about care, facilities, treatment skills and clinical research.

Mission Patient-Centered
Innovation

Our goal is to make joint care understandable and accessible,
supporting daily activity through considered treatment and continued learning.
