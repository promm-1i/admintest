ORVELLA Fellowship

ORVELLA Orthopaedic Care
Fellowship Program

The Orvella fellowship connects supervised clinical observation with structured discussion, practical skills and research. Participants consider how assessment, treatment and rehabilitation contribute to a patient’s daily life.

Orvella Knee Fellowship

Program Overview

Seoul, South Korea Site

This fellowship combines supervised clinical learning with individual teaching in knee arthroplasty and arthroscopy. It is intended for orthopaedic clinicians who plan to develop these interests in their continuing practice. Domestic and international applicants can discuss the program requirements with the education team.

Participants learn with the knee care team under the guidance of Dr. Doyun Kim. Clinical teaching focuses on understanding the reasons for a treatment choice, preparing for a procedure and reviewing recovery. Case discussion and direct supervision provide opportunities to connect knowledge with everyday patient care.

Fellowship Schedule

Work and Training

Training covers the assessment of knee conditions and relevant approaches, including arthroscopy, ligament reconstruction, osteotomy and joint replacement.

The six-day clinical schedule includes ward review, outpatient assessment and the stages of rehabilitation after treatment.

Supervised clinic and operating-room activities help participants understand patient care across different settings.

Weekly Case Discussions

Research activity

Research and academic discussion form part of the learning program alongside clinical training. Participants join a research group supervised by Dr. Doyun Kim and the education team, with time to consider a clinical question, review relevant literature and discuss an appropriate study method. Weekly meetings provide an opportunity to present work in progress, ask questions about interpretation and receive feedback. Each participant agrees a project with the supervisors and reviews its scope and timetable during the fellowship. The emphasis is on careful observation, responsible use of clinical information and clear reporting, rather than a guaranteed publication outcome. Where suitable opportunities are available, participants may share their work at an educational meeting and attend seminars or practical workshops. These activities support continued learning and thoughtful discussion with colleagues working in the same field.

Applications can be reviewed throughout the year. Placement dates depend on the available teaching capacity and the number of participants already scheduled, so a requested start date cannot be confirmed until the program review is complete.

Next Steps Selection process

The review considers clinical preparation, learning goals and communication skills under a consistent, fair selection process. Applicants who are selected are contacted with the proposed placement details. The team then explains the documents and practical arrangements required for arrival, including any visa-related information that needs to be checked.
