Help

The phone can't find the PC

Check that the phone and the PC are on the same Wi-Fi (the same router) and that the Byphoto icon is in the PC's notification area. If the firewall question in PC step 3 wasn't allowed, the phone can't find the PC. If it still doesn't show up, type ipconfig in the PC's Command Prompt and enter its IPv4 Address in Connect by address.

What if the PC is off?

The photos stay on the phone and go at the next backup after the PC is on. A sleeping PC can't receive them, so keep it awake while it backs up.

Outside access says it doesn't work

Turning on UPnP in your router's settings often fixes it. A home with a second router behind the provider's modem usually can't be reached from outside. Backing up and viewing on your home Wi-Fi still work.

Does it do videos?

Yes. Videos in the chosen albums go to the PC as originals, and they're marked when you browse your PC's photos on the phone. Tap one to download the original and open it on the phone.

I want to switch to another PC

On the app's home, tap ⋮ at the top right → Connect another PC, and enter the new PC's number. Photos the old PC received stay on it.

I want to report a problem

Type %LOCALAPPDATA%\Byphoto in File Explorer's address bar and email us the byphoto.log file there. It records what happened; it never contains your photos.
