FAQ

VECTORGRID robots are built from modules, report their status live and find their own way with smart navigation. Together these let a company run its warehouse more smoothly, get more done each day and spend less on running costs.

Yes. VECTORGRID equipment fits into an ordinary warehouse with only small changes to the site.

Because the system is built from modules, a business can grow simply by adding robots, storage units or new workflows. Work stays efficient through each stage of growth, and there is no need to tear out what is already in place.

VECTORGRID pairs capable robots with solid software that plugs easily into existing systems, so work is accurate, quick and easy to change. Its systems serve online retail, grocery, pharmaceutical and 3PL businesses, and many warehouses rely on them for their automation.
