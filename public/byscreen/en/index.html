Your tablet as a second monitor for your PC

Your Android tablet becomes display 2 in Windows. Connect it with a USB cable or over the same Wi-Fi; touch and pen input on the tablet go to the PC.

Display 2 is the tablet. Drag it to a different side of the laptop. Do the same in Windows Settings and the pointer crosses over on that side.

Connection

One cable, or the same router. A tablet you've allowed once connects by itself the next time you plug it in or open the app.

USB cableFastest and steadiest. Needs a cable that carries data and USB debugging turned on in the tablet.
Recommended
Wi-FiThe PC and tablet just need to be on the same router. Office and café networks often keep devices apart, so a cable is better there.
Extend or duplicateAdd the tablet as a new screen, or show a monitor you already have. Choose from the tray menu at the bottom right of the PC.

Refresh rate

Byscreen sends as many frames as the tablet's screen can show, up to 120 a second. Every row below moves the same bar at the same speed; only the frames per second differ.

Pen & touch

Touching the tablet is touching the PC. Turn these on or off in the tablet app's settings.

Pen pressureHow hard you press reaches drawing and note apps on the PC.
On by default
Palm rejectionFingers are ignored while the pen is near the screen.
On by default
Trackpad modeFingers move the pointer like a laptop touchpad; the pen still touches where it lands.
Off by default

Off by default

Help

The app asks me to turn on USB debugging

The PC program uses USB debugging to switch the tablet to cable mode. Follow tablet step 2.

Nothing happens when I plug in the cable

Charge-only cables don't connect; try a USB-C cable that carries data. Check that the PC program is running in the tray.

My work PC asks for an administrator password

The two one-time setups (new screen, USB driver) need administrator rights. Ask your IT team; after that it doesn't ask again.

I want to report a problem

Type %LOCALAPPDATA%\Byscreen in File Explorer's address bar and email us the host.log file there. It records the tablet model and what happened.
