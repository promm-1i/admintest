Help

The app says "Turn on USB debugging"

The PC program uses USB debugging to switch the tablet over to the cable. Follow tablet step 2.

The app says "Press Connect on the PC"

Choose Connect on the card at the bottom right of the PC's screen. If there's no card, start Cross on the PC and plug the cable in again. A card closed without an answer asks again next time.

Nothing happens when I plug the cable in

Charge-only cables don't connect. Try a USB-C cable that carries data, and check that the PC program is in the notification area.

I use Byscreen too

One cable serves one app at a time. Open the app you want on the tablet and press "Switch to Cross" or "Switch to Byscreen". Next time you plug in, the last app you used connects.

A work PC asks for an administrator password

Installing the USB driver the first time needs administrator rights. Ask your IT team; it won't ask again after that.

I want to report a problem

Type %LOCALAPPDATA%\Cross into File Explorer's address bar and email us the cross.log file there. It records what happened.
