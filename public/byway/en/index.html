Help

It says "No street map for this area yet"

Byway makes routes in all of Korea and in New York, London, Tokyo, Paris, Los Angeles, San Francisco, Sydney, Toronto, Singapore and Berlin. Other places don't have a street map yet.

It says "This size doesn't fit the streets here"

The nearby streets don't make that shape at that distance. Tap Change distance, or try another shape.

It says "Couldn't download this area's street map"

The first route in an area needs a connection to download its street map. Connect and try again. Areas you've used before work offline.

Does recording stop when the screen is off?

No. While Byway's notification is showing, it records and announces turns. If notifications are off, you won't see progress there. It stops when you tap Finish or arrive.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Turn off Voice guidance or Vibration in Settings. Voice speaks 80 m before each turn; vibration comes 20 m before it, two short for right and one long for left.

I bought Plus on another phone

On a phone signed in to the same Google account, open Byway's Settings and tap Restore purchase.
