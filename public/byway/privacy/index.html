개인정보처리방침

시행일 2026년 11월 23일. MintCL(이하 "⁠운영자")⁠은 바이웨이 앱(이하 "⁠바이웨이")⁠을 운영하면서 아래와 같이 개인정보를 처리합니다.

  • 걸은 길은 폰 안에만 저장됩니다길 만들기와 따라가며 기록하기는 폰 안에서 하고, 걸은 기록과 내 그림은 운영자에게 보내지 않습니다.
  • 폰 밖으로 나가는 것은 지도를 받는 데 필요한 만큼길 지도와 지도 그림을 받을 때 그 지역의 칸 번호가, 동네 이름을 찾을 때 지금 위치가 나갑니다. 운영자는 이것을 저장하지 않습니다.
  • 계정·광고·분석 도구 없음가입이 없고, 광고, 광고 식별자, 사용 분석 도구를 넣지 않았습니다.

1. 바이웨이가 하는 일

바이웨이는 하트·별·글자 같은 모양을 지금 있는 곳 근처의 실제 길 위 경로로 만들고, 사용자가 그 길을 따라 걷거나 달리거나 자전거로 가는 동안 지나간 길을 기록하는 앱입니다. 위치는 길을 만들 때와 [이 길로 출발]⁠을 누른 뒤 길을 따라가는 동안에만 씁니다.

2. 폰 안에만 남는 정보

  • 걸은 기록: 만든 길, 따라가며 기록한 위치(시각·정확도), 걸은 거리와 시간, 고른 모양, 동네 이름. '⁠내 그림'⁠에 모이며, 거기서 지우거나 앱을 지우면 함께 지워집니다.
  • 받은 길 지도: 한 번 길을 만든 동네는 인터넷 없이 쓸 수 있게 그 지역의 길 지도 파일을 폰에 둡니다.
  • 설정: 소리 안내, 진동 안내, 거리 단위, 바이웨이 플러스 구매 여부.
  • 사진: [사진 저장]⁠을 누르면 폰의 사진 폴더(Pictures/Byway)⁠에 저장됩니다. [공유하기]⁠를 누르면 사용자가 고른 앱으로 사진이 넘어가고, 그 뒤는 그 앱의 방침을 따릅니다.

이 정보는 운영자에게 오지 않습니다.

Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026. MintCL ("we") runs the Byway app ("Byway"). This policy explains what data Byway handles.

1. What Byway does

Byway turns a shape such as a heart, a star or letters into a route on real streets near you, and records where you go while you walk, run or ride along it. Location is used only while a route is being made and, after you tap [Start this route], while you follow it.

2. Data kept only on your phone

  • Your walks: the route, the locations recorded along it (with time and accuracy), distance, time, the shape and the neighborhood name. They appear in My drawings and are deleted when you delete them there or uninstall the app.
  • Downloaded street maps: once you make a route in an area, its street-map files stay on the phone so the area works offline.
  • Settings: voice and vibration guidance, distance unit, and whether Byway Plus was bought.
  • Photos: [Save photo] saves the picture to your phone's Pictures/Byway folder. [Share] hands it to the app you choose, whose own policy applies from then on.

None of this is sent to us.

3. Data that leaves your phone

  • Our street-map file server (Cloudflare, Inc., United States, served from a nearby data centre): the numbers of the roughly 4 km map squares around you and your IP address, over HTTPS, the first time you make a route in an area, so the street-map files can be sent. We don't turn on request logging and don't store these requests.
  • OpenFreeMap (Hyperknot Software Kft., Hungary): the numbers of the map squares on screen and your IP address, over HTTPS, when the map is shown or a share photo is made, to send the map images. OpenFreeMap says it keeps no IP addresses in its access logs and deletes those in error logs after 7 days (OpenFreeMap privacy policy).
  • Your phone's address lookup (Android Geocoder, usually provided by Google, United States): the coordinates of your location, over HTTPS, when a route is made and again after you've moved more than 300 m, to show the neighborhood name. Covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Map-square numbers identify a square of the map, not an exact spot. We don't link any of this to you or store it.

4. Data we process

Your email address and message when you contact us, to answer you. Kept for one year after we reply. We collect no other personal data.

5. Permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate): to make a route on nearby streets, record where you go while you follow it, and tell you about turns. Byway doesn't ask for background location.
  • Foreground service (location): to keep recording and turn guidance going with the screen off after you tap [Start this route]. A notification shows the whole time, and it stops when you tap [Finish] or arrive.
  • Notifications: to show that recording is on, with the distance drawn and left.
  • Vibration: to signal turns.
  • Internet: street maps, map images, neighborhood names and purchase checks.

6. Payments

Byway Plus is sold through Google Play. We never receive card or payment details; we see the order details Google shows sellers in Play Console (order number, product, time, country). Google's handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Sharing

We don't sell or share personal data, except where the law requires it.

8. Ads and analytics

Byway has no ads, no advertising ID, no analytics and no crash reporting.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or to stop its processing, by emailing us. Walks on your phone are deleted with [Delete] in My drawings or by uninstalling the app. Our servers keep nothing per user.

10. Security

Every request that leaves the phone uses HTTPS. Your walks are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read.

11. Children

Byway is not directed at children.
