1. 바이캠이 하는 일과 정보가 오가는 곳

바이캠은 안드로이드 폰의 카메라를 USB 케이블로 연결한 윈도우 PC의 웹캠으로 쓰게 하는 앱입니다. PC 프로그램이 PC에 "바이캠" 카메라를 만들고, 줌·팀즈·OBS·크롬 같은 PC 앱이 그 카메라를 고르면 폰 카메라가 켜집니다.

다음 정보는 폰과 PC 사이의 USB 케이블로만 오갑니다. 운영자의 서버나 다른 회사를 거치지 않습니다.

  • 카메라 영상(소리 없음): PC 앱이 바이캠 카메라를 쓰는 동안만 보냅니다. PC 앱이 카메라를 닫으면 폰 카메라도 꺼집니다.
  • 연결 정보: 폰의 제조사와 모델명, 앱 판 번호, PC의 컴퓨터 이름, PC 앱이 고른 해상도와 초당 프레임.

PC로 간 영상을 PC 앱(화상 회의, 방송 프로그램 등)이 녹화하거나 인터넷으로 보내는 것은 그 앱과 사용자의 선택이며, 그 앱의 개인정보처리방침을 따릅니다.

2. 기기 안에만 남는 정보

  • 폰: 앞·뒤 카메라 선택, 해상도 선택(720p·1080p), 1080p를 결제했는지(Google Play에서 확인한 값), Google Play가 알려 준 현지 가격.
  • PC: 바이캠이 쓰는 폰을 다른 MintCL PC 프로그램과 구분하기 위해, 폰의 USB 일련번호를 PC의 윈도우 레지스트리(현재 사용자 영역)에 적어 둡니다. 문제를 찾을 때 쓰도록 연결·끊김 시각, 폰의 USB 장치 경로(일련번호 포함), 초당 프레임 같은 전송 상태를 PC의 작업 기록 파일에 남깁니다.

바이캠은 영상을 저장하지 않습니다. 폰의 정보는 바이캠 전용 저장 공간에 있고 앱을 지우면 함께 지워집니다. 사용자가 안드로이드 백업(구글 계정)을 켜 두었다면 이 설정이 그 백업에 함께 저장될 수 있습니다. 이 정보는 운영자에게 오지 않습니다.

Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026. MintCL ("we") runs the Bycam app and PC program ("Bycam"). This policy explains what data Bycam handles.

1. What Bycam does and where data goes

Bycam lets you use your Android phone's camera as the webcam of a Windows PC it is plugged into with a USB cable. The PC program adds a "Bycam" camera to the PC, and the phone camera turns on when a PC app such as Zoom, Teams, OBS or Chrome picks that camera.

The following goes only over the USB cable between your phone and PC. It doesn't pass through our servers or any other company.

  • Camera video (no sound): sent only while a PC app is using the Bycam camera. When the PC app closes the camera, the phone camera turns off.
  • Connection details: the phone's maker and model, the app version, the PC's computer name, and the resolution and frame rate the PC app chose.

Once the video is on your PC, whether a PC app (video calls, streaming software and so on) records it or sends it over the internet is up to that app and you, under that app's privacy policy.

2. Data kept only on your devices

  • Phone: front or back camera, chosen resolution (720p or 1080p), whether 1080p was bought (as confirmed by Google Play), and the local price Google Play reported.
  • PC: to tell Bycam's phone apart from other MintCL PC programs, the PC program writes the phone's USB serial number in the PC's Windows registry (current user). For troubleshooting, it keeps a log file on the PC with connect and disconnect times, the phone's USB device path (which includes the serial number) and stream status such as frame rate.

Bycam never stores video. The phone data sits in Bycam's private app storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app. If you turned on Android backup (your Google account), these settings may be included in that backup. None of it reaches us.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: to send the phone camera's video to your PC.
  • Foreground service (camera), wake lock: to keep the cable connection and video going while the screen is off or you use other apps. A notification shows while this runs.
  • USB accessory: to connect to the PC program over the cable.
  • Google Play billing, internet, network state: used only to buy 1080p and to check the purchase and price with Google Play. The internet and network state permissions come with Google Play's billing library; Bycam sends no video or other data over the internet.

5. Payments

The in-app purchase (1080p, one-time) is handled by Google Play. Card numbers and other payment details never reach us. In Google Play Console we see only the order details Google shows sellers, such as the order number, product, time and country. Google's handling of data follows the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Processors and transfers abroad

We don't hand Bycam's personal data to processors or transfer it abroad. The camera video never leaves the PC it is plugged into.

7. Sharing

We don't share personal data with anyone, except when required by law.

8. Ads and analytics

Bycam has no ads, advertising ID or usage analytics.

9. Deletion

Data is deleted without delay once its retention period ends, by methods that can't be undone.

10. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal data by emailing us below; we will act without delay. Phone data goes when you uninstall the app. The PC records sit in the registry key HKCU\Software\MintCL\Accessory and the %LOCALAPPDATA%\Bycam folder, and you can delete them yourself.

11. Security

The camera video and connection details travel only over the USB cable, never over a network. The phone sends video only to a connection the Bycam PC program requested and you allowed on the phone.
