Help

They tapped Show but I can’t see the screen

After Show, Android asks once more: they need to tap Start or Share screen there. If that prompt was closed, ask again from your phone.

No notification on their phone

If their phone is off or has no internet, the request can’t reach it. Also check that Bycare’s notifications are on in their phone’s settings (parent’s phone 2).

Tapping for them doesn’t work

When tapping for them is off on their phone, your phone offers pointing only (parent’s phone 4). After the first 3 days it takes the one purchase on your phone. It also stops while a banking or payment app is in front.

It stops after 30 minutes

Without the purchase you can look for 30 minutes at a time. Ask again to carry on.

How do we unpair?

On either phone, tap the family member’s name and choose to disconnect. To pair again, scan a new QR code while you are together.

Is the screen stored anywhere?

No. The screen, the taps and the text are end-to-end encrypted between the two phones on their way through our relay, which can’t read them. Details in the privacy policy.
